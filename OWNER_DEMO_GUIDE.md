### 5. How to Present to the Owner

1. **Option A: Live Walkthrough in Browser (Most Impressive)**
   - Open: `http://localhost:5173/demo`
   - Select either **Hindi (हिंदी)** or **English**.
   - Press **Play Video Presentation**. The presentation will progress smoothly through all 5 scenes with synchronized captions, animations, and voiceover.
2. **Option B: One-Click Jump to Any Feature**
   - Click any scene tab (1 to 5) to jump directly to that part of the presentation while talking to the owner.
3. **Option C: Direct Audio & Video Files for Video Editors**
   - Use the download buttons on `/demo` or grab the files from `client/public/assets/audio/`.
